About

I'm finishing my bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the Faculty of Applied Sciences, University of West Bohemia. For my thesis I'm combining Wave Function Collapse with ant colony optimization to generate levels in Unity. After graduating I want to go on to the master's programme in Software and Information Systems.

I also teach at SPŠ Tachov: programming, application development and practical training. With fourth-year students I work on their final project, which they then defend in front of an examination board as part of their maturita, the Czech school-leaving exam.

I want to work as a software engineer. The projects I enjoy most are the ones I keep using after they're done, like ARIA, a desktop dashboard I built around my own study and coding routine.
